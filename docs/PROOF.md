## How to read the public-photo rows

Composites, not phone captures: a real object cut out of a public photograph by code and pasted on our rendered screen mat at a scale set from one published dimension (the long side). That dimension is marked set_by_construction and excluded from every error. The mat, the camera and the light on the mat are rendered, the cut-out is flat, has no walls and casts no shadow. Counted as 0 real photos measured. It says nothing about a phone photographing a laptop screen.

Measures how well a flat-plane marker fit holds on real lenses and real printed paper. It says nothing about a phone photographing a laptop screen, nothing about measuring a part, and these photos are not counted as real photos measured. The boards' printed size is unknown, so all numbers are relative; the mm figure is the relative error applied to a 100 mm span on our mat, not a measured length.

scale-1: the documented layout fits only 24/80 outer corners, so it does not describe this print (0.9292 % held-out as written). The outer markers alone give a gap ratio of 0.2 (0.0794 % held-out). That photo is left out of the headline because its layout is estimated, not documented.

In the full loop, the largest error of a final model on the excluded scale dimension was 13.54 mm (aa_cell-1). Those rows stay in each run's `score.json` under `eval/runs/live/public_photos/`.

The first measure-only pass had a mean abs error of 0.599 mm (n = 20) with cast shadows inside some cut-outs. The masks were then fixed by eye against the object's edge, and the numbers above are from after that fix. The first pass is kept in `eval/reports/latest/public_photos_measure_first_pass.json`.
